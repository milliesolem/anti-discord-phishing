
# Anti Discord Phishing

**TL;DR** *This repo will teach you and your loved ones how to not get hacked over Discord, and what to do if you do. All of this is hand-written by a human cybersecurity professional.*

In this repo you will find resources for protecting yourself against Discord token grabbers, browser stealers and other nastiness. __If you are the guardian of a child using Discord,__ I implore you to carefully study this repository and especially consider implementing pre-emtive measures on your child's devices.

The year 2026 has seen a rapid increase in malware spread over Discord, only exasterbated by the increased availability and efficiency of AI coding tools. Opportunist cybercriminals motivated by money and human depravity use these tools to develop and deploy malware that employ advanced techniques such as deep presistence in the operating system, automated extraction and decryption of top-shelf password managers and browser data, and automated targeting of and spreading to emerging targets.

These are techniques that were previously too advanced and time-consuming to develop for anyone but cybersecurity professionals and nation state intelligence to implement and use, AI makes them ubiquitous in the form of tailor-made malware automatically generated for free in minutes from a single request. This serves as a major threat for private individuals, who are largely not prepared to resist a targeted cyberattack of this form.

In response to this, I have made this repository in hopes it will help educate and enable victims and the wider public to effectively mitigate and recover from these attacks. I want to level the playing field between you and the hacker.

#### Why does this exist?

Short answer: to help victims, and to draw attention to this pressing matter.

Long answer:

The run-of-the-mill security advice of 2FA/MFA, password-managers and anti-virus scans usually dished around won't fully cut it here, as these attacks by design target the blind-spots of these mechanisms. I see a severe lack of public awareness on how to stay safe, corroborated by the sheer volume of victims, which is why I made this.

## Pre-emptive measures and awareness

The key thing to bear in mind is that everyone makes mistakes. You never know when they will come for you, it could be when you're stressed, tired, busy, sleep-deprived, or otherwise in a distracted headspace where mistakes are made. Nobody is immune to manipulation or simply a bad day. Awareness and humbleness to this fundamental limitation of the human condition helps you not drop your guard.

The number one measure against this is educating yourself on exactly how phishing campaigns on Discord are performed. I've compiled a list of the 5 most common traits of a phishing attack over Discord.

### Red flags
1. A stranger with few or no common servers sends you a friend request
2. A friend you know asks if you want to join a Minecraft server or try a game.
3. You're sent a Minecraft mod or link to an indie/mobile game.
4. Time-pressure to download, click, run or install something.
5. Repeated inquiries about your computer's specs.

## Post-infection response & recovery

**Turn your computer off now!** An infected computer will be actively monitored and controlled by the attackers; they will change your online passwords and sift through your local files looking for kompromat.
